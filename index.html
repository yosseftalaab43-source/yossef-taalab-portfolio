About me

Turning learning into action.

I am a youth trainer and community-development practitioner from Menoufia, Egypt. My work focuses on facilitating learning experiences, supporting youth participation, and contributing to initiatives around personal development, employability, entrepreneurship and social awareness.

My approach is practical: understand the people and their needs, create an engaging learning environment, and connect every activity to a clear next step.
